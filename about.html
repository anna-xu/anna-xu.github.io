About Me

Hi, thanks for stopping by my "About Me" section!

I'm Anna, a current PhD Candidate/Researcher & Data Science Scholar alum @ Stanford University. My email is xua!at!stanford!dot!edu (delete the exclamation marks). Look below for more info ↓

My PhD research involves analyzing MRI data to build more precise human brain models of neural plasticity induced by learning. I'm particularly drawn to the parcellation question of how to functionally divide the brain into regions - essentially reducing the high dimensionality of MRI scans into meaningful brain parcels.

Aside from my dissertation work, I've also previously worked as a research intern at Toyota Research Institute in Human-Centered AI, with a focus on carbon neutrality and applied behavioral science.

Prior to Stanford, I worked as a data analyst at Penn Lifespan, Informatics, and Neuroimaging Center, using meta-analyses to evaluate MRI for biomarker development in chronic pain. Before that, I graduated from Brown University with a BSc in cognitive neuroscience, conducting research on the effects of performance feedback on fatigue and effort (advised by Amitai Shenhav).
